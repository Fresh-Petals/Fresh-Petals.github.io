Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Scope of this notice

This Fresh Petals privacy notice currently covers Shubh Panchang for Android. It does not cover PinkPortrait or other Fresh Petals products unless they expressly link to this notice.

Fresh Petals ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This notice explains how Shubh Panchang handles information when you use the app.

Information the app uses

  • Location: If you choose the GPS location option and grant Android location permission, the app uses your device coordinates to choose a nearby city and calculate Panchang information. This processing happens on your device. The app saves the selected city and its coordinates on your device; if no matching city is available, it can save the coordinates returned by GPS. Shubh Panchang does not send your GPS coordinates to Fresh Petals.
  • App settings: The app stores your selected city and locale, language, notification preference and time, onboarding status, and a local premium-status indicator on your device.
  • Notifications: Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. The app stores the reminder preference and time; Fresh Petals does not receive notification content or a notification history from the app.
  • Purchases: If you purchase Premium, Google Play Billing processes the transaction. The app receives the purchase result and keeps a local premium-status indicator. Fresh Petals does not receive your payment card details through the app.

Advertising and service providers

Shubh Panchang integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When ads are enabled, Google may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics to provide, measure, and protect advertising services. The information Google processes depends on your device and ad settings and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's user-consent policies.

Google Play Billing handles in-app purchase processing. These Google services may collect and retain information under their own privacy notices and terms. We do not operate an account system or a server backend for Shubh Panchang.

How information is stored and deleted

App preferences and location settings are stored locally on your device. You can remove this local app data by clearing Shubh Panchang's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Shubh Panchang does not create user accounts, so there is no app account to delete. Purchase records and information handled by Google are subject to Google's retention and deletion processes.

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and any information you include in your message so we can respond.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke location permission in Android settings and select a city manually in the app. You can change or turn off reminders in the app's Settings. You can manage advertising preferences through the controls offered by Google and your device.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the app or its data practices change. We will publish the current version on this page and update the effective date.

Contact

For questions about this notice or Shubh Panchang privacy, contact Fresh Petals at freshpetals2018@gmail.com.
