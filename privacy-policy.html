Apps and permissions

Shubh Panchang — Android permissions and data use

Location and notification permissions

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: used only after you choose GPS location and grant permission. Coordinates are processed on-device to select a nearby city and calculate Panchang information; location is not accessed in the background.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: lets the app request permission to show local daily reminders on Android 13 and later. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: lets the notification plugin reschedule local reminders after a device restart.
  • VIBRATE: allows the device to vibrate for notifications.

Network, ads, billing, and library permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: allow the app and its Google ads and billing libraries to connect to their services and check network availability.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: enables Google Play Billing for Premium purchases.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS: included by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising ID and Android ad attribution/Topics APIs on supported devices. Google may use related information for ad delivery and measurement, subject to your device and ad settings.
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE: included by Google Play services measurement and background-work libraries for their platform functions. They do not give the app background location access.
  • An app-specific DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION: an internal signature-level permission added by AndroidX to protect app receivers. Its name is derived from the app's package ID; it is not a user-granted permission.

Library permissions and Android ad APIs can vary with platform and dependency versions. The list above reflects the current merged Android release manifest.

Information used and stored

The app stores your selected city and coordinates, city locale, app language, notification preference and time, onboarding status, and a local premium-status indicator on your device. If no city matches your GPS position, the app can store the coordinates returned by GPS. Shubh Panchang does not send your GPS coordinates to Fresh Petals.

Daily reminders are scheduled locally. Fresh Petals does not receive notification content or a notification history from the app. The app does not create user accounts or use a Fresh Petals server backend.

Third-party services

When ads are enabled, Google Mobile Ads may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics to provide, measure, and protect advertising services. Google Play Billing processes Premium purchases; the app receives the purchase result and saves a local status indicator. Fresh Petals does not receive payment card details through the app. Google's processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's user-consent policies.

Retention and deletion

For Shubh Panchang, preferences and location settings remain on your device until you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. The app does not create user accounts, so there is no app account to delete. Purchase records and information handled by Google are subject to Google's retention and deletion processes.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke location permission in Android settings and select a city manually in Shubh Panchang. You can change or turn off reminders in the app's Settings. You can manage advertising preferences through the controls offered by Google and your device.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the app or its data practices change. We will publish the current version on this page and update the effective date.
